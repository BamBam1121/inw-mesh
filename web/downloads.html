LilyGo T-Deck and T-Deck Plus

Made for the touchscreen: your newest chats on the home screen, swipe up to unlock, tap to answer. Version 1.2.4.

Install in your browser Download the .bin

On Wi-Fi it updates itself; beta updates in Settings › System gets new builds before they're released. The .bin is one image, written at address 0x0 with esptool or Espressif's web flasher: how.

Which one do I have? The pager is the wide one with a scroll wheel on its side. The T-Deck is shaped like a small BlackBerry, with a trackball above the keyboard. Coming from other firmware? Each install page says how to keep your keys and contacts first.

What changed in each version: releases. The source: GitHub (GPL-3.0).
