themes / your own

Make your own theme

Pick your colours, give it a name, and put it on your pager or T-Deck over USB. It shows up in Settings → Theme next to the built-in ones. Everything happens in this browser and on your device: nothing is uploaded, and nobody else sees it.

1 Start from a look

The look decides the lock-screen picture, the shapes, the sounds and the buzz. The colours are yours.

2 Choose your colours

Click a colour to change it. Point at one to see where it is used.

Or try one:

Every colour (10 more)

These follow your main colours until you set one yourself. Tick auto to let it follow again.

3 Name it

Whatever you like, up to 20 characters. Plain letters, numbers and signs: the menu's font has no accents or emoji.

4 Put it on your device

Plug your pager or T-Deck into this computer with a USB cable and switch it on. It restarts once when the browser connects, which is normal, and nothing on it is lost.

Sending over USB needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. You can still design here: copy the link below and open it in Chrome or Edge to send it.

    Good to know
    
  
    
    
    Up to four of your own. Send one with a name you have used before and it
    replaces that one. Connect and you can take any of them off again from this page.
    
    
    Light themes work. Pick a pale background and dark text: names, bubbles and
    the rest follow. If writing gets hard to read, the page says so.
    
    
    It is only on your device. Other people on the mesh see your messages,
    not your colours. A theme is 15 colours and a name, kept in the device's settings.
