How can we help?

Most answers are in the guide. If you're still stuck, have an idea, or found a bug, tell us: every report gets read.

Get unstuck

The guide

Keys, first start, updates, battery, notifications and the SD card.
Flashing problems

No port showing up, won't connect, or won't start after installing.
Common questions

Cost, devices, keeping your contacts, the phone app and licences.

Talk to us

Questions, bugs and ideas live on GitHub, where anyone can search earlier answers. You'll need a free GitHub account to post.

Ask a question

Setup help, "how do I...", or anything that isn't a bug.
Report a bug

Something isn't working right on the pager or in the installer.
Suggest a feature

An idea that would make the pager more useful to you.
Show your setup

Share your pager, antenna, repeater or a spot with great range.

A good bug report includes

  • Your firmware version: Settings → System → version
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened
  • Whether it happens every time, and how to make it happen
  • A photo of the screen, if something looks wrong

Keep secrets out of posts

Everything on GitHub is public. Never post an exported backup, a private key, or a private channel's name or secret. Anyone holding those can read your messages or pretend to be you.
