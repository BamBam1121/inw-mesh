Support Squatch Mesh

Squatch Mesh is free and open source, and it stays that way. If it made your pager or T-Deck better, you can chip in here. It goes to test hardware (the T-Deck port was built without one on the desk) and to keeping this site and the help desk running.

Payments go through Ko-fi, by card or PayPal. This site never sees your card. It's a tip to the developer, not a tax-deductible donation.

Other ways to help

Tell someone on your mesh about it. Report a bug or an idea on the help page. Star it on GitHub. All of it helps as much as money does.
