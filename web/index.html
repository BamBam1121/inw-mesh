Squatch Mesh turns your T-Deck, pager or M9 into a standalone off-grid messenger: channels, direct messages, maps and repeater tools, right on the device. No phone, no internet, no subscription.

Messaging
Channels, direct messages and rooms, with delivery ticks, retries, @mentions and emoji. Room for 2,000 contacts and 40 channels.
Offline maps
Map tiles from the SD card, with every node that shares its position drawn on top.
Repeater tools
Log in to repeaters, read status and telemetry, trace routes and see which repeaters carried a message.
Battery that lasts
GPS and the screen power down when you're not looking, and a saver kicks in when low. On the pager, charging holds at 80%.
Safe Wi-Fi updates
Releases are signed and checked before installing, and your contacts and keys are left alone.
Phone app & NFC
Pair with the MeshCore app over Bluetooth. On the pager, tap a phone to share your contact or a channel.

No towers. Just radios passing it on.

Your device sends a short, encrypted burst over LoRa radio. No SIM, no account. Radios on hills hear it and pass it on once each, so it spreads across the region without looping. Miles away someone's radio chimes, and replies find the quickest way back.

More about the mesh

Questions

Not answered here? Get help or send feedback.

Which devices does it run on?

The LilyGo T-Deck and T-Deck Plus (the same firmware runs on both; only the Plus has GPS built in), the LilyGo T-Lora Pager, and the Elecrow ThinkNode M9. The mesh itself is MeshCore, so you can talk to anyone on a MeshCore network, whatever device they use.

Does it cost anything?

No. Squatch Mesh is free and open source under GPL-3.0, and the mesh has no subscription or fees. You only need the device.

Will I lose my contacts when I update?

No. Updates over Wi-Fi or from the website keep your contacts, channels, messages and settings. Only tick "erase device" in the installer if you mean to wipe everything.

Does it still work with the MeshCore phone app?

Yes. Pair it over Bluetooth like any MeshCore companion, and the phone and the device share the same contacts and settings.

Do I need a ham radio licence?

No. MeshCore uses unlicensed ISM radio bands, so no callsign is needed. Stay within your region's power limits.

What if something goes wrong while flashing?

Run the installer again: it writes everything the device needs to start, even on a blank chip. If the device no longer shows up, its install page says how to put it in flash mode: T-Deck, pager (hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT), M9.

Put it on your device

Flash Squatch Mesh from your browser in a few minutes. Free, open source, and yours to change. It's made in the Inland Northwest and tested on the local MeshCore mesh, and more than 150 devices have run it so far.
