Squatch Mesh

Firmware for the LilyGo T‑Deck, T‑Lora Pager and ThinkNode M9 that lets you text people on the MeshCore mesh right from the device. You don't need a phone or cell service for it.

I started it on my T‑Lora Pager here in the Inland Northwest, then ported it to the T‑Deck and the M9. It's free and open source (GPL-3.0), and once it's on there it updates itself over Wi‑Fi.

Install it

Or go straight to your device: T‑Deck & T‑Deck Plus, T‑Lora Pager, ThinkNode M9

Installing

It installs from Chrome or Edge in about two minutes. Plug the device in with a USB-C cable that does data (charge-only cables are the number one reason it doesn't show up), click Install and pick the port.

Updating later keeps your contacts, channels and messages, so you can try a new version without starting over. The Inland Northwest radio preset is set up out of the box, and any other MeshCore settings work too.

What it does

Everything runs on the device. The MeshCore phone app still works with it if you want it.

Messaging
Channels, DMs and rooms, with delivery ticks, retries, @mentions and emoji. It holds 2,000 contacts and 40 channels.
Offline maps
Map tiles off the SD card, with everyone who shares their position drawn on top.
Repeater tools
Log into repeaters, check their status and telemetry, trace routes, and see which repeaters carried your message.
Battery
GPS and the screen shut off when you're not using them, and a saver kicks in when you're low. The pager stops charging at 80% to go easy on the cell.
Updates
Releases are signed and checked before they install, and they leave your contacts and keys alone.
Phone app and NFC
Pairs with the MeshCore app over Bluetooth. On the pager you can tap a phone to share your contact or a channel.

How the mesh works

Your device sends a short encrypted burst over LoRa radio. Repeaters on hills and rooftops hear it and pass it along once each, so it spreads across the area without bouncing around forever. That's how a message gets miles away without any cell towers involved.

Who makes this

Me. It's a one-person project from the Inland Northwest, tested every day on the local MeshCore mesh, including a couple of repeaters I run myself. More than 150 devices have run it so far.

If something's broken or you want a feature, use the help page or come find me in #squatch-mesh on the Inland NW Mesh Discord.

Questions

Which devices does it run on?

The LilyGo T-Deck and T-Deck Plus (same firmware, only the Plus has GPS built in), the LilyGo T-Lora Pager, and the Elecrow ThinkNode M9. The mesh itself is MeshCore, so you can talk to anyone on a MeshCore network whatever they're using.

Does it cost anything?

Nothing. It's GPL-3.0 and the mesh has no fees. You just need the device.

Will I lose my contacts when I update?

No. Updates from the site or over Wi-Fi keep your contacts, channels, messages and settings. Only tick "erase device" in the installer if you actually want a clean slate.

Does the MeshCore phone app still work?

Yes. Pair it over Bluetooth like any MeshCore companion and the phone and the device share contacts and settings.

Do I need a ham licence?

No. MeshCore runs on the unlicensed ISM band, so there's no callsign. Just stay under your region's power limits.

What if flashing goes wrong?

Run the installer again. It writes everything the device needs to boot, even on a blank chip. If the device doesn't show up at all, its install page says how to get it into flash mode: T-Deck, pager (hold BOOT, tap RESET, let go of BOOT), M9.
