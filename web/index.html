How a mesh works

01

You send

Your pager transmits a few hundred bytes over LoRa. No tower, no SIM, no account.

02

Repeaters relay

Nodes on hills hear it and pass it on. Each one relays a given packet once, so it spreads without looping.

03

It arrives

Miles away, someone's pager buzzes. The reply comes back the same way, often by a shorter direct path.

What it does

Messaging

Channels, DMs, rooms. Delivery ticks, retries, repeats heard, @mentions, emoji.
Map

Offline SD tiles, every node that shares a position, tiles fetched over Wi-Fi.
Repeater tools

Login, status, telemetry, trace route, remote console, discovery, sniffer.
Themes

Four, each with its own scene, sounds, vibration and charging indicator.
Battery

An accurate percentage, optimised charging, and a saver that kicks in at 20%.
Wi-Fi updates

Signed releases, verified before install, and the old version stays if anything fails.
NFC

Tap a phone to share a contact or hand over a channel invite.
Phone app

Still pairs with the official MeshCore app over Bluetooth.

Put it on your pager

Flash it from the browser over USB. Chrome or Edge, no tools to install, and it never touches your contacts on an update.
