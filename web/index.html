What it does

A full messenger on the device itself

Everything runs on the pager. The phone app is optional.

Messaging

Channels, direct messages and rooms, with delivery ticks, retries, @mentions and emoji.
Offline maps

Map tiles from the SD card, with every node that shares its position drawn on top.
Repeater tools

Log in to repeaters, read status and telemetry, trace routes and see which repeaters carried a message.
Battery that lasts

GPS and the screen power down when you're not looking, charging holds at 80%, and a saver kicks in when low.
Safe Wi-Fi updates

Releases are signed and checked before installing, and your contacts and keys are left alone.
NFC & phone app

Tap a phone to share your contact or a channel, and pair with the MeshCore app over Bluetooth.

How it works

No towers. Just radios passing it on.

You send

Your pager transmits a short, encrypted burst over LoRa radio. No SIM, no account.

Repeaters relay

Radios on hills hear it and pass it on once each, so it spreads across the region without looping.

It arrives

Miles away someone's pager buzzes, and replies find the quickest way back.

Questions

Frequently asked

Not answered here? Get help or send feedback.

Which devices does it run on?

The LilyGo T-Lora Pager. The mesh itself is MeshCore, so you can talk to anyone on a MeshCore network, whatever device they use.

Does it cost anything?

No. Squatch Mesh is free and open source under GPL-3.0, and the mesh has no subscription or fees. You only need the pager.

Will I lose my contacts when I update?

No. Updates over Wi-Fi or from the website keep your contacts, channels, messages and settings. Only tick "erase device" in the installer if you mean to wipe everything.

Does it still work with the MeshCore phone app?

Yes. Pair it over Bluetooth like any MeshCore companion, and the phone and pager share the same contacts and settings.

Do I need a ham radio licence?

No. MeshCore uses unlicensed ISM radio bands, so no callsign is needed. Stay within your region's power limits.

What if something goes wrong while flashing?

Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, and run First Install again. It writes everything the pager needs to start, even on a blank chip. See the install page.
